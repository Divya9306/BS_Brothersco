About Us

B.S. Brother's is a leading manufacturer of custom steel and iron utility products, serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Haryana. Founded in 1999, we've built our reputation on three pillars: exceptional craftsmanship, reliable service, and innovative design solutions. Our state-of-the-art fabrication facility combines traditional metalworking expertise with modern welding technology to deliver products that exceed industry standards. From intricate decorative gates to heavy-duty industrial hoarding boards, every piece we create reflects our commitment to precision, durability, and aesthetic excellence.
